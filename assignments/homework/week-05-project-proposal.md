---

## Purpose

This assignment is a progress checkpoint, not a polished deliverable. The goal is to make sure your group has settled on a focused, feasible question and has started engaging with the data before you invest significant effort in the midterm project itself.

You will use the provided notebook template to write a short proposal and do some preliminary exploration of the Complete Journey dataset. Your instructor will review submissions and provide brief feedback — including approval of your question or a suggestion to refine it — before Week 6.

## Instructions

1. Open the notebook template using the Colab link below:

## Grading (10 points)

This assignment is graded on genuine effort, not perfection. A group that engages seriously with all sections earns full credit.

**Criterion 1: Completeness (4 points)**

| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 4 | All sections are completed with substantive responses. Answers reflect genuine group thinking — not placeholder text, one-liners, or copy-pasted examples from the template. |
| 2 | Most sections are completed but one or two are thin, vague, or left as placeholder text. |
| 0 | Multiple sections are missing or filled with placeholder text only. |

**Criterion 2: Business Question (3 points)**

| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 3 | The question is specific, answerable with the Complete Journey data, and tied to a clear business decision or decision-maker. |
| 1 | The question is too broad, too vague, or only loosely connected to the data or a business context. |
| 0 | No meaningful question is stated, or the question cannot be addressed with the Complete Journey data. |

**Criterion 3: Preliminary Exploration (3 points)**

| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 3 | Code goes beyond loading the data — includes at least one meaningful filter, aggregation, join, or visualization relevant to the proposed question. Written summary reflects what was found. |
| 1 | Some code is present but amounts to only basic inspection (e.g., `.head()`, `.info()`, `.describe()`). Written summary is missing or does not connect to the proposal. |
| 0 | No code beyond the data loading cells provided in the template, or code cells are empty. |

**Note:** Your instructor may leave comments on your submission. If your question needs refinement before you proceed, you will be asked to resubmit a revised Section 2 only — this will not affect your grade as long as it is completed promptly.
